## Setup

- **Model**: `emotion2vec_plus_large` as released (no fine-tuning), FunASR 1.4.16, one prediction per clip.
- **Data**: 22,584 clips of THAI-SER (Thai) from `data/thai_ser/`.
- **Ground truth (acted emotion)**: the emotion assigned to the actor for the scene (`assigned_emo`).
- **Listeners' vote**: the crowd-sourced majority label (`majority_emo`); clips without a majority have none.
- Label mapping: Neutral = neutral, Angry = angry, Happy = happy, Sad = sad. Clips assigned *Frustrated* (5,270 of 27,854) are left out because emotion2vec+ has no such class; a listener vote of *frustrated* can therefore never match.
- Audio: the condenser microphone for studio sessions and the Zoom recording for online sessions (44.1 kHz FLAC, resampled to 16 kHz).
- **4 emotions**: the prediction is the most probable of the 4 emotions in this dataset (neutral, angry, happy, sad); the model's other classes (`disgusted`, `fearful`, `other`, `surprised`, `<unk>`) are ignored. **All 9 classes**: the prediction is the most probable of all nine classes, so choosing a class that the dataset does not have counts as wrong.
- **Two ways to count a prediction as correct.** *Acted emotion*: it must match the acted emotion. *Acted emotion or listeners' vote*: it also counts when it matches the listeners' majority vote (for a tied vote, any of the tied emotions). Both are reported.
- **Hardware**: Apple M4 Max, device `cpu`, PyTorch 2.5.1. Inference took 52.5 min (140 ms per clip).
- **Per-clip predictions**: `outputs/thai_ser_emotion2vec_plus_large_predictions.csv` (not in git; re-run the script to recreate it). The JSON file next to it describes the columns.

## Predictions outside the 4 emotions

With all nine classes, the model chose a class that this dataset does not have for 2,591 clips (11.5%): `disgusted` 1,168, `fearful` 323, `other` 126, `surprised` 509, `<unk>` 465.

## Comparison with human listeners

The listeners' majority vote matches the acted emotion for 64.6% of the 20,466 clips with a single (untied) vote. The model agrees with that vote for 54.6% of the same clips.

## Caveats

- **Not in EmoBox**, so emotion2vec+ seed was not fine-tuned on it. emotion2vec+ large was later trained on 40k hours of pseudo-labelled data whose sources are not published, so earlier exposure cannot be ruled out completely.
- **Labels often disagree.** The assigned and the crowd-majority labels differ for many clips; the dataset authors recommend the high-agreement subset (agreement >= 0.71) for clean comparisons.
- Some sessions were recorded in an untreated studio room or over Zoom, so audio quality varies.
- **Acted labels are not perceived emotions.** The ground truth is what the actor intended; listeners sometimes heard something else, as the listeners' numbers above show.
