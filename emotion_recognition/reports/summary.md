- **†**: the dataset is in the model's training data, so the model has probably seen these clips; treat the result as optimistic.
- **Bold**: the best model on the dataset.
- **Subsets**: THAI-SER (high-agreement subset), MEAD (8,000-clip subset): a fixed subset of the dataset (`subsets` in config/evaluation.yaml), the same for every model; each report says how it is chosen.

## Training data

- [emotion2vec+ large](emotion2vec_plus_large/summary.md): EmoBox (through emotion2vec+ seed). In its training data: CREMA-D, RAVDESS, TESS, JL-Corpus, MEAD (8,000-clip subset), MELD, EMOVO, Emozionalmente, SAVEE.
- [MERaLiON-SER-v1](meralion_ser_v1/summary.md): CREMA-D, ESD, MELD, M3ED and Singaporean speech. In its training data: CREMA-D, MELD.
- [Melati](melati_emotion/summary.md): nine acted corpora, JL-Corpus among them. In its training data: JL-Corpus, Emozionalmente.
- [Vox-Profile Whisper](vox_profile_whisper/summary.md): MSP-Podcast. None of these datasets is in its training data.
- [Vox-Profile WavLM](vox_profile_wavlm/summary.md): MSP-Podcast. None of these datasets is in its training data.
- [Gemma 4 E4B](gemma4_e4b/summary.md): not published (Google). Whether these datasets are in its training data is not known.
