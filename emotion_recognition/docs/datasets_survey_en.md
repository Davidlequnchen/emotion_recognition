# Emotion Recognition Public Dataset Survey

English translation of [datasets_survey.md](datasets_survey.md) (Chinese); the two have the same content.

Survey date: 2026-10-23. Goal: survey public emotional speech datasets that can be used to evaluate emotion recognition
models (emotion2vec+) and audio large language models (audio LLMs).
The initial selection favoured the same sentences spoken in different emotions to reduce lexical clues. We now also
adopt dialogue audio with speakers, transcripts and verified turn order to study how past context changes a judgment.
Compare these designs separately and check label quality, scale, licences and access.

Update checked 2026-11-10: the project now adopts and retains **16 datasets**, including **DailyTalk, EmotionTalk and MultiDialog**. The original 13 have baseline results from five SER models. The three additions are downloaded, audited and published through the data repository Release; **their baselines remain pending**. MultiDialog retains only two official test splits; its default complete gold-actor-pair plus dialogue-QC cohort has 7,672 clips (section 3c). IEMOCAP and MSP-Conversation remain later candidates, neither downloaded nor adopted (section 3a).

Update 2026-11-13: all 16 datasets are evaluated. The three dialogue datasets now have baseline results from the five SER models, GPT Realtime, Gemini 3.8 Flash and Qwen2.5-Omni 7B; Qwen2-Audio and Gemma 4 are still pending. The current numbers are in [reports/summary.md](../reports/summary.md).

Method: searched papers, official project pages, GitHub, Hugging Face, Zenodo, OSF and similar sources, and collected the
information on each dataset.
Sizes are in decimal GB, from the file listings of each platform's API or from HTTP requests; "about" marks an estimate,
and "not published" means the platform gives no size.
"Unverified" marks numbers that could not be confirmed from a primary source. For the datasets downloaded locally
(see section 6), the number of clips, the duration and the sampling rate were measured after downloading.

**Legend (used throughout):** ✅ = adopted and retained by this project (16 datasets; section 0). As of 2026-11-13 all 16 have SER baselines, including DailyTalk, EmotionTalk and MultiDialog. 🗄️ = evaluated earlier, now out of scope (only ESD); no mark = not adopted.

## 0. Key points

- **Separate EmoBox association from confirmed use by the selected checkpoint.** CREMA-D, ESD, MEAD, RAVDESS, TESS, SAVEE, EmoDB, CASIA, Emozionalmente, SUBESCO, JL-Corpus, CaFE, EMOVO and EmoV-DB appear in EmoBox. This indicates potential family/teacher association, not proof that the selected emotion2vec+ large trained on each corpus.
- **Emotion2vec family provenance:** seed uses 201 h of academic EmoBox data without a precise subset list; base uses seed-generated pseudo-labels; large uses 42,526 h of undisclosed in-house industrial pseudo-labelled data. Original emotion2vec pretraining includes IEMOCAP, MELD, CMU-MOSEI, MEAD and MSP-Podcast. Paper-supported teacher/upstream propagation counts as involvement in the training process; this does not establish that every raw recording directly trained the selected large checkpoint. The source audit preserves the propagation chain and its limits.
- **Candidates absent from the EmoBox inventory:** MCAE-SPPS, THAI-SER, MSP-IMPROV target sentences and nEMO. This does not certify exclusion from every model/backbone. The [eight-model × 16-corpus audit](training_provenance.md) covers retained backbones and fine-tuning. Figures use one marker for supported training-process involvement; no marker does not mean verified unseen.
- **English**: no dataset was found that is large, text-parallel, perceptually validated and not in EmoBox all at once; the only option is to combine MSP-IMPROV, MESS and Expresso.
- **Dialogue-context datasets**: **DailyTalk ✅ (English), EmotionTalk ✅ (Mandarin) and MultiDialog ✅ (English, QC-limited)** are adopted. Their single-utterance baselines are done (as of 2026-11-13); the next step is testing the effect of past turns on the same target audio (sections 3b and 3c). IEMOCAP and MSP-Conversation remain later candidates requiring access applications. The former offers utterance categories and dimensions; the latter mainly supplies continuous V/A/D traces over long conversations, so it is not the same seven-class task (section 3a).
- **Evidence for this approach**: [LISTEN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10444) (2025) tested audio LLMs on the text-neutral sentences of CREMA-D, ESD, TESS, SAVEE and RAVDESS.
  With audio only, accuracy was just 16.5–34.9% ([Qwen2.5-Omni](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20215) 34.0%, [Gemini 2.5 Pro](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06261) 34.9%); with text only it was 85–97%.
  This is consistent with a stronger reliance on lexical semantics; scores alone do not establish the internal mechanism or show that audio is ignored.

**Training markers:** direct training, upstream pretraining, seed fine-tuning and teacher pseudo-label propagation all count as used/seen. Explicit author development calibration is also counted with its stage. Supported upstream propagation also counts as seen; the main table lists model names without grade letters. See the [shared audit](training_provenance.md).

- **Subsets** (decided 2026-11-06, to save compute; `subsets` in `config/evaluation.yaml`): every model is evaluated on the same fixed subset of two datasets. THAI-SER: only the clips with agreement ≥ 0.71 (the share of the crowd raters who chose the majority label), the subset the dataset authors recommend: 11,848 clips (happy 3,665, neutral 2,999, angry 2,883, sad 2,301). MEAD: 8,000 clips at even intervals over the clips sorted by actor, emotion, intensity level and sentence, so each of the 912 actor/emotion/level cells keeps its share (3 to 12 clips). Hours measured from the audio file headers.
- **Original-13 corpora absent from the public SER training lists**: MCAE-SPPS, MESS, THAI-SER and nEMO. Supported EmoBox/original-emotion2vec upstream associations count as training-process involvement, without claiming direct raw-recording use by plus-large. Melati author threshold calibration uses nEMO and is counted as seen in development. Absence does not exclude backbone, pseudo-label or audio-LLM training exposure; DailyTalk, EmotionTalk and MultiDialog are not claimed to be strictly unseen either.
- **Size convention**: the main table sums retained file bytes under `data/<dataset>/` in the 2026-11-09 snapshot, with MultiDialog added on 2026-11-10 (decimal GB, including existing local caches and legacy copies), rather than download size or filesystem allocated blocks. The three additions match their published manifests. THAI-SER now retains four-class mirror audio and metadata: **4.640 GB** in files, about **4.69 GB** allocated by `du`; the full 12.71 GB original Parquet is no longer retained. nEMO still includes Parquet, extracted cache and legacy transfer copies locally.
- **Baselines for the additions**: DailyTalk defaults to ECSS validation 1,197; design any formal dialogue holdout separately. EmotionTalk retains all 19,250 utterances; its official test contains 1,929, including 1,830 eligible dyadic-history targets with completed past turns. MultiDialog defaults to 7,672 clips/353 complete dialogues after both-gold-actor and whole-dialogue QC; all 18,141 source recordings remain retained. These are the evaluated scopes; as of 2026-11-13 the five SER models, GPT Realtime, Gemini 3.8 Flash and Qwen2.5-Omni 7B have results on them.
- **ESD 🗄️ is out of scope**: its licence is research-only, forbids any transfer to third parties and lists "testing commercial systems" as commercial use. The data is still on disk; the old emotion2vec+ result is in `reports/archive/`.
- **SAVEE ✅ comes from an unofficial Kaggle copy**: the official corpus is free for research after registration, and its licence forbids redistribution (§2.2), so the Kaggle copy is not an authorised mirror. Its clips never go on a public page (`public_audio: False`); the original 13 retain their existing research API settings. The three dialogue datasets follow the same research API setting (`api_audio: true`), and GPT Realtime and Gemini 3.8 Flash results exist for them.
- MELD is not parallel text (TV dialogue), so it is not in the survey tables of sections 1–3.

**Why this ranking:**

1. **MCAE-SPPS**: a thoroughly parallel design, with perceptual labels from 40 listeners and intensity ratings for every clip; its 200 sentences have far more lexical variety than CREMA-D's 12;
   its 7 classes match those of emotion2vec+ exactly. Released only in 2025, and no record was found of any audio LLM trained or evaluated on it.
   Drawbacks: only 6 speakers; NC licence (no commercial use).
   Citation: Li M, Zhou A, Yan H, Li Q, Ma C, Wu C. *Scientific Data*, 2026, doi:10.1038/s41597-026-06976-z; data doi:10.17605/OSF.IO/9JYZC.
2. **THAI-SER**: the most speakers, crowd-sourced perceptual labels with agreement, two intensity levels, and the most permissive licence.
   Drawbacks: the scripted part has only 3 sentences; "frustrated" has no counterpart in emotion2vec+, so using only neutral, angry, happy and sad is recommended.
   [VoxEmo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08936) (2026) has already used it for zero-shot evaluation of [Qwen2-Audio](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10759) and [AF3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08128) (evaluation only, no training). Citation: arXiv:2507.09618.
3. **MSP-IMPROV**: the most established English data with "controlled text + crowd-sourced perceptual labels + not in EmoBox".
   Drawbacks: the controlled-text part has only about 1.3k clips, an agreement must be signed, and [OSUM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13306) has used it as a test set. Citation: Busso et al., IEEE TAC 8(1):119–130, 2017.
4. **nEMO**: new data from 2024, fully parallel, direct download; its emotions are close to CREMA-D's (no disgust, but surprise).
   Drawbacks: small, recorded at home; VoxEmo has evaluated on it. Citation: Christop, LREC-COLING 2024, pp. 12111–12116.
5. **HCUDB**: 34 professional actors and 11,220 clips, with the same sentences recorded in many emotions; HCUDB1 has dimensional ratings, useful for intensity analysis.
   Drawbacks: mapping its 11 classes to emotion2vec+ needs compromises; the application process is in Japanese.
6. **MCAE pseudo-sentences**: word meaning is removed completely, so a model can only judge by the voice; the best test of "does the model really listen"; two intensity levels. Drawback: fewer clips than CREMA-D.
   Citation: Gong et al., *Behavior Research Methods* 55(3):1441–1459, 2023.
7. **MESS**: a small English supplement, parallel, CC BY licence. Drawbacks: "calm" instead of "neutral", only 4 classes; the public version has acted labels only.
   Citation: Morgan, JSLHR 62(11), 2019.
8. **MASC**: the only Mandarin data with "many speakers + large + parallel", useful to test generalisation across speakers. Drawbacks: 8 kHz, no listening test, paid.

**What ground truth means here.** Both provide human perceptions of emotion, not directly verified internal emotional states. IEMOCAP can support classification after fixing category and agreement rules. The full 10,039 turns are not the 5,531-turn four-class subset; the original paper also reported incomplete dimensional annotation at that time, so check completeness after access. MSP-Conversation provides three evolving curves: valence (negative to positive), arousal (calm to active) and dominance (weak to strong). It supports dimensional regression and dynamics; arbitrary thresholds do not create official angry/happy labels.

**MSP-Conversation counts and public labels.** The **12,555 speaking turns** are the intersection with MSP-Podcast, not the total number of utterance clips or a verified count of overlap with our models' training sets. The corpus is organised as long conversations; the number of extracted clips depends on turn/window definitions. Partitions are Train 169 conversations (41 h 22 min), Dev 52 (12 h 36 min), Test1 58 (14 h 17 min), Test2 31 (9 h 9 min). Test2 labels are withheld for external evaluation. Conversation versus Podcast comparisons also change continuous/utterance-level annotation and scales; differences cannot all be attributed to context. [2026 paper, section V](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.22536v1)

**Storage budget is not download size.** Public official pages give no verifiable archive byte count, and we have not obtained the data. Keep the GB field as not published. For planning only, one copy at **16 kHz, mono, PCM16** would occupy about **1.38 GB for 12 h**, or **8.92 GB for 77 h 26 min** (duration × 16,000 × 2 bytes). These calculated converted-audio sizes are **not archive sizes** and exclude video, motion data, duplicated sessions/clips and intermediate files.

IEMOCAP licence clause 6 also calls for consultation with the provider before planned evaluations, comparisons and public reporting; describe this project's research use when applying. MSP-Conversation requires an authorised institutional signature rather than personal click-through access. If integrated later, configure API transfer and sharing according to the terms actually granted, rather than copying current datasets' settings. [Detailed checks and examples](../data/emotiontalk/README.md)

**What related papers use.** [ECSS, AAAI 2024](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.11947) directly uses and reannotates DailyTalk for emotional conversational speech; [EmotionTalk, Findings ACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.440/) supplies richer audio-perception labels. **QC is a disclosed scope restriction.** All WAV headers, PCM payloads, metadata pairs and contiguous turns passed. Source-only checks nevertheless found implausible audio/text segmentation. Before running any model or inspecting predictions, we froze two rules: flag a clip if it has at least six words and more than eight words per second, or if it exceeds 30 seconds with fewer than 0.6 words per second. Fully unpacked, verified ZIPs have been removed. The original-source downloader uses `unpacked.json` to record extracted files and normally redownloads only when files are missing.
The original-source THAI-SER/nEMO route uses Parquet (`pyarrow`) and caches evaluation WAVs: THAI-SER chooses the studio condenser microphone or online Zoom recording. The current THAI-SER Release mirror directly retains selected FLACs of the four evaluated classes, without the full original Parquet. nEMO's mirror retains its two original Parquet files and extracts audio for evaluation.
