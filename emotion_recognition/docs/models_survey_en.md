# Emotion Recognition Open-Source Model Survey

English translation of [models_survey.md](models_survey.md) (Chinese); the two have the same content. The dataset survey is in [datasets_survey_en.md](datasets_survey_en.md).

Survey date: 2026-10-28. Goal: survey speech emotion recognition (SER) models similar to emotion2vec+ large whose weights can be downloaded and run locally, and pick the ones worth evaluating next in this project.
Selection criteria: downloadable weights, with code or documentation on how to run them (non-commercial licences are included but flagged); English supported (multilingual is fine); any size; the whole emotion2vec family is included.

Method: queried the Hugging Face and ModelScope APIs (file lists and sizes, licences, creation dates) and read the model cards, papers (arXiv) and GitHub code.
Parameter counts: for safetensors, the figure from the Hugging Face API, or counted from the tensor shapes in the file header; for repositories with only .pt, .bin or .ckpt files, counted from the tensor shapes read out of the checkpoint with HTTP range requests. No weights were downloaded. A count covers the network itself; anything else in the file (optimizer state, an unused decoder) is noted in brackets.
Sizes are in decimal MB and GB. Parameter counts: M = million parameters, B = billion parameters; one decimal below 1B, two decimals from 1B up, and "about" where the source gives only an approximate value. The year is the month the weights or the paper first appeared. "Unverified" marks what could not be confirmed from a primary source.

## 0. Key points

- **emotion2vec+ large really has 162.1M parameters**, not the "~300M" on its card; about two thirds of its 1.95 GB file is optimizer state. seed and base both have 93.2M.
- **What exists besides emotion2vec+:**
  - Dimensional output (arousal, valence, dominance): audEERING, the Odyssey 2024 baselines, Vox-Profile, MERaLiON-SER and LAION all have it; emotion2vec+ does not.
  - Other backbones: WavLM-large (about 316M) is the most common; Whisper encoders (small, medium, large-v3) are increasingly used; the smallest is a CNN with only 0.016M parameters (Wav2Small). In EmoBox's comparison of frozen backbones, the Whisper large-v3 encoder ranks first on 23 of 32 datasets, followed by WavLM-large.
  - Licences: commercial use is allowed for the Odyssey baselines (MIT), SpeechBrain and SUPERB (Apache-2.0), MERaLiON-SER (MIT plus attribution terms), Aniemore (MIT) and LAION (CC BY 4.0), and emotion2vec+ itself does not restrict it; audEERING, Vox-Profile, Melati and Audio Flamingo 3 are non-commercial only.
  - Sizes: dedicated SER models mostly have 93.2M to 639.9M parameters; open-weight audio LLMs have 5.54B to 35.26B.
- **Several new models get their labels from emotion2vec+**: SenseVoice's emotion labels were produced by emotion2vec+ large, and parts of MERaLiON-SER's and C²SER's training data were labelled with emotion2vec+. They are not independent references.
- **Most comparable to emotion2vec+ large**: MERaLiON-SER-v1 (exactly this project's 7 classes, plus dimensional output, commercial use allowed), Melati (7 classes, Whisper large-v3, non-commercial), and Vox-Profile (9 classes) and the Odyssey 2024 baseline (8 classes), both trained on MSP-Podcast only.
  Melati's card compares three of them on 7,791 held-out clips (nEMO and the VoxCeleb part of NonverbalTTS), UAR: emotion2vec+ large 0.522, MERaLiON-SER 0.464, Melati 0.538.
- **Worth evaluating next** (the models marked ⭐ in the tables; see section 5 for the effort):
  1. MERaLiON-SER-v1: exactly this project's 7 classes, plus dimensional output, commercial use allowed; loaded with transformers, a few dozen lines of code.
  2. Melati: 7 classes, slightly above emotion2vec+ large on its card's held-out data; non-commercial, also loaded with transformers.
  3. The Vox-Profile categorical models (Whisper, WavLM) and the Odyssey 2024 categorical baseline: trained on MSP-Podcast only (natural emotion from podcasts). Vox-Profile is the speech-only version of the best system of the IS2025 challenge, but non-commercial and it needs its own package, and its card says predictions for clips shorter than 3 s are unreliable; the Odyssey baseline is MIT-licensed and the easiest to load.
  4. The dimensional models (audEERING, Vox-Profile WavLM dimensional, Odyssey 2024 dimensional): they output arousal, valence and dominance, which needs a separate analysis.
  - emotion2vec+ seed, base and SenseVoiceSmall are the easiest to add (configuration only, or a small change), but they share their origin with emotion2vec+ large, so they say little as a comparison and are not marked ⭐.

## 1. Dedicated SER models with categorical output

Table 1a has the basic facts and the reported performance, table 1b the architecture, data and output; the # numbers match.

⭐ = recommended to evaluate first: good reported performance, output classes close to this project's 7, downloadable and runnable, then the licence; only the names of recommended models are in bold.

Metrics: WA is accuracy, UA (UAR) is the average of the per-class recalls (fairer when the classes are unbalanced), macro-F1 is the average of the per-class F1 scores, and CCC is the agreement of a dimensional prediction with human ratings (1 is best). The numbers in different rows come from different datasets and protocols and cannot be compared across rows; only emotion2vec+ large has been measured on this project's datasets.

## 5. Effort to add a model to this project

`scripts/emotion2vec/evaluate.py` loads the model with FunASR's `AutoModel`, reads the 9 classes from `tokens.txt` and takes the class probabilities (`scores`) that `generate()` returns, which it maps to each dataset's classes. A model from another framework needs its own loading code.

| Effort | Models | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| Configuration only | emotion2vec+ seed, base; emotion2vec base finetuned | register under `models:` in `config/config.yaml`, download and switch `model:`, as in `models/README.md`. All three output the same 9 classes |
| Small change | SenseVoiceSmall | also loaded with FunASR, but `generate()` returns text with tags: the tag must be turned into a class, and there are no per-class probabilities (unless the CTC logits of the emotion tokens are read) |
| A transformers loader (a few dozen lines) | MERaLiON-SER-v1, the Odyssey 2024 baseline, Melati (with `trust_remote_code`); Aniemore, SUPERB and the community models of section 1c (standard `AutoModelForAudioClassification`) | a `Predictor` that returns class probabilities, plus a mapping from the model's class names to this project's 7 (e.g. `ang` → angry, `Happiness` → happy). The project pins transformers 5.19.0, so remote code written for 4.x may need changes (untested) |
| Extra packages or code changes | Vox-Profile (vox-profile-release, loralib; the Whisper version only runs on a Mac once the hard-coded `.cuda()` is removed), the two SpeechBrain models, autrainer, ParaCLAP; Emotion2Vec-S and emotion2vec base output features only, so a classification head must be trained first | whether the new dependencies can coexist with FunASR 1.4.16 and transformers 5.19.0 is untested |
| A different evaluation | the dimensional models of section 2; the audio LLMs of section 4 | this project's datasets have class labels only, so dimensional outputs need another analysis (for example, the distribution of each emotion on arousal and valence); the audio LLMs can reuse the prompts and parsing of `scripts/llm_eval/`, but need local GPU inference |

## 6. Notes

- **Non-commercial or restrictive licences**: the two audEERING models and autrainer (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), Vox-Profile (OpenRAIL, but the card rules out commercial use), Melati (research only), Audio Flamingo 3 and AF-Next (NVIDIA non-commercial), Qwen2.5-Omni-3B (Qwen Research). ParaCLAP's licence is unclear (just "cc"), and its audio branch comes from an NC model.
  Community uploads often label models trained on NC data (such as RAVDESS) as Apache-2.0 or MIT; such labels are unreliable. SALMONN needs Vicuna-13B v1.1, which is restricted by the LLaMA licence (details unverified).
- **Parameter counts on cards that do not match the files**: emotion2vec+ large (card ~300M, actually 162.1M); MERaLiON-SER (card 309M, measured 313.4M without the decoder, file 770.0M, the difference being the unused Whisper decoder); Vox-Profile's Whisper models (file 1.55B, also with the decoder). About two thirds of every emotion2vec .pt file is optimizer state.
- **Chains of pseudo-labels**: SenseVoice (emotion2vec+ large), MERaLiON-SER (emotion2vec+ seed and base) and C²SER's internal data (emotion2vec+ large) were all labelled with emotion2vec+, and the sources of emotion2vec+ base's and large's own pseudo-labelled data are not published. These models' errors may correlate with emotion2vec+'s.
- **Domain and clip length**: the MSP-Podcast models learnt natural emotion from podcasts. Vox-Profile dropped clips shorter than 3 s in training, and its card says predictions for such clips are unreliable.
- **Creation dates on Hugging Face**: 2022-03-02 is the date shared by all repositories migrated from the old system, not a release date; the years of SUPERB, SpeechBrain IEMOCAP and ehcalabres are inferred from the cards and library versions.
