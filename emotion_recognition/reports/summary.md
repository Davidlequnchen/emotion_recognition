- **Training involvement**: † Used directly; ‡ Used via upstream training; ? Possible association; no marker No corpus-specific evidence. ## Training data

- [emotion2vec+ large](emotion2vec_plus_large/summary.md): EmoBox academic data is used for seed fine-tuning, followed by teacher pseudo-labelling and data iteration to large; this project counts those corpora as used through upstream training. Large also uses 42,526 h of industrial pseudo-labelled audio. This is distinct from a claim that every EmoBox corpus directly entered large encoder self-supervised pretraining. Used via upstream training: CREMA-D, RAVDESS, TESS, JL-Corpus, MEAD (8,000-clip subset), MELD, EMOVO, Emozionalmente, SAVEE. No corpus-specific evidence: MCAE-SPPS, THAI-SER (high-agreement subset), nEMO, MESS.
- [MERaLiON-SER-v1](meralion_ser_v1/summary.md): SER training on CREMA-D, ESD, MELD, M3ED and Singaporean speech; retained Whisper-medium pretraining sources are incompletely disclosed; upstream seed/base teacher use is counted as seen. Used directly: CREMA-D, MELD. Used via upstream training: RAVDESS, TESS, JL-Corpus, MEAD (8,000-clip subset), EMOVO, Emozionalmente, SAVEE. No corpus-specific evidence: MCAE-SPPS, THAI-SER (high-agreement subset), nEMO, MESS.
- [Melati](melati_emotion/summary.md): Public SER head trained on nine named corpora including JL-Corpus and Emozionalmente; Whisper-v3 pretraining incompletely disclosed; author nEMO threshold calibration is counted as seen development use, although its thresholds are not applied here. Used directly: nEMO, JL-Corpus, Emozionalmente. No corpus-specific evidence: CREMA-D, MCAE-SPPS, THAI-SER (high-agreement subset), MESS, RAVDESS, TESS, MEAD (8,000-clip subset), MELD, EMOVO, SAVEE.
- [Vox-Profile Whisper](vox_profile_whisper/summary.md): SER adaptation on MSP-Podcast v1.12; retained Whisper-large-v3 pretraining sources incompletely disclosed. No corpus-specific evidence: CREMA-D, MCAE-SPPS, THAI-SER (high-agreement subset), nEMO, MESS, RAVDESS, TESS, JL-Corpus, MEAD (8,000-clip subset), MELD, EMOVO, Emozionalmente, SAVEE.
- [Vox-Profile WavLM](vox_profile_wavlm/summary.md): SER adaptation on MSP-Podcast v1.12; WavLM-large backbone trained on Libri-Light, GigaSpeech and VoxPopuli; no clip-level overlap audit. No corpus-specific evidence: CREMA-D, MCAE-SPPS, THAI-SER (high-agreement subset), nEMO, MESS, RAVDESS, TESS, JL-Corpus, MEAD (8,000-clip subset), MELD, EMOVO, Emozionalmente, SAVEE.
- [Qwen2.5-Omni 7B](qwen2_5_omni_7b/summary.md): Qwen2.5 and Whisper-large-v3 initialization plus multimodal pre/post-training; complete corpus-level provenance not published. No corpus-specific evidence: MELD.
- [Qwen2-Audio 7B Instruct](qwen2_audio_7b/summary.md): Qwen-7B and Whisper-large-v3 initialization; report states evaluation-data exclusion, but full backbone and pre/post-training corpus provenance is unavailable. No corpus-specific evidence: MELD.
- [Gemma 4 E4B](gemma4_e4b/summary.md): Broad multimodal pretraining and instruction tuning; complete corpus-level provenance not published (Google). No corpus-specific evidence: MELD.
