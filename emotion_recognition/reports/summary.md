## Training data

- [emotion2vec+ large](emotion2vec_plus_large/summary.md): EmoBox (through emotion2vec+ seed). In its training data: CREMA-D, RAVDESS, TESS, JL-Corpus.
- [MERaLiON-SER-v1](meralion_ser_v1/summary.md): CREMA-D, ESD, MELD, M3ED and Singaporean speech. In its training data: CREMA-D.
- [Melati](melati_emotion/summary.md): nine acted corpora, JL-Corpus among them. In its training data: JL-Corpus.
- [Vox-Profile Whisper](vox_profile_whisper/summary.md): MSP-Podcast. None of these datasets is in its training data.
- [Vox-Profile WavLM](vox_profile_wavlm/summary.md): MSP-Podcast. None of these datasets is in its training data.
